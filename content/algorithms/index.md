## Purpose

This section holds algorithmic tools that recur across many problems. The notes split into two rough groups: core concepts such as [[algorithms/graphs-intro|graph fundamentals]], [[algorithms/tree-intro|trees]], and [[algorithms/induction|induction]], and reusable patterns such as [[algorithms/patterns/BFS|BFS]], [[algorithms/patterns/sliding-window|sliding windows]], and [[algorithms/dynamic-programming|dynamic programming]].

If you are starting from scratch, read [[algorithms/graphs-intro|graph fundamentals]] before the graph traversals, then move to [[algorithms/BFS|breadth-first search]], [[algorithms/DFS|depth-first search]], and [[algorithms/connected-components|connected components]]. For counting arguments and proofs, [[algorithms/induction|induction]] and [[algorithms/tree-intro|trees]] carry a lot of the load.

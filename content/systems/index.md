## Purpose

This section groups the computer systems material by subject rather than by source. The main branches are [[systems/operating-systems/index|operating systems]], [[systems/networks/index|networks]], [[systems/distributed-systems/index|distributed systems]], [[systems/databases/index|databases and data-intensive systems]], [[systems/performance/index|performance engineering]], and [[systems/research/index|systems research]].
